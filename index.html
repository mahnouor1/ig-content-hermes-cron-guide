The 8AM Cron Lead Engine

One chat message sets it up. Every morning at 8 AM, fresh leads are already waiting in my CRM, without me lifting a finger.

The idea

Most people use AI to answer questions. I used it to start work that keeps running while I sleep.

I tell my AI agent once:

“

Every morning at 8 AM, find me 20 new leads. ”

It turns that sentence into a cron job: a task that runs on its own, at the same time, every day. From then on, my lead engine finds businesses, collects their contact details, checks them, removes anyone I already have, and drops the fresh list into my CRM.

The AI only understands the request. Everything after that is a system I built.

Why it works

AI talks, code decides.

The agent only turns my words into a job. Who counts as a lead, what's a duplicate, and what's verified are fixed rules, so the results are the same every day.

The agent has one job.

It can schedule a search and report back. It can't send emails or touch anything else, so it can't go off script.

A human stays in charge.

The system fills the CRM. Contacting anyone is always my call.
